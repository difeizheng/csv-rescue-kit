# 24-Hour CSV Cleanup — Experiment Offer

**Fixed launch price: US$29.** This is a test offer, not a claim of typical market pricing or guaranteed availability.

## You receive

1. One cleaned CSV, up to 10,000 rows and 30 columns.
2. A concise report of rows removed, values standardized, and issues requiring your review.
3. One revision for corrections within the agreed cleaning rules.
4. When useful, the reusable Python cleaning configuration or script.

## Included in the fixed scope

- Consistent column names and surrounding whitespace cleanup
- Empty-row and exact-duplicate removal
- Lowercased email addresses and invalid-email flags
- Agreed date-format standardization
- Before/after row counts and an audit report

## Four-workbook consolidation option

For up to four Excel workbooks with consistent columns, a fixed **US$49 experimental package** includes one master `.xlsx`, selected columns such as name/date/amount, exact-duplicate removal, and a reconciliation report. The public `excel_consolidate.py` sample demonstrates this workflow using fictional data.

## Not included

- Guessing missing values or silently correcting uncertain records
- Legal, medical, financial, or other regulated-data advice
- Bypassing access controls or acquiring data you are not authorized to share
- Complex dashboards, multi-file reconciliation, or ongoing data pipelines

## Safe intake

[Open a public service inquiry](https://github.com/difeizheng/csv-rescue-kit/issues/new?template=service-inquiry.yml) for initial scope only; private files require a separately agreed private channel. Send a fictional or redacted sample of 10–20 rows first. Do not send passwords, API keys, payment-card data, government IDs, or unredacted sensitive personal data. Agree on the cleaning rules before full delivery.

## Delivery target

Within 24 hours after scope and sample are confirmed. The original file is never overwritten. No order, buyer, or revenue exists yet; this page documents the first market experiment.
